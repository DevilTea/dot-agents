# Shopping preferences

- For **physical-product purchase recommendations**, exclude brands under effective control or material strategic decision-making influence of PRC-linked capital. China-based manufacturing or non-controlling minority investment alone does not disqualify a brand.
- If ownership or controlling influence cannot be reasonably verified, omit the brand from **primary recommendations**; present it separately as unverified if relevant. Never quietly relax eligibility rules.
- This purchase filter does not prevent factual investigation of an excluded product, nor does exclusion itself imply security, privacy, or quality misconduct. Use the `product-research` skill for deeper comparisons when available.

These preferences apply by default when choosing, evaluating for purchase, or recommending **physical products**. They do not prohibit factual research into an excluded brand or product.

## Brand ownership and control (confirmed)

- Exclude brands under effective control or material strategic decision-making influence of capital originating from the People's Republic of China from the primary recommendation set.
- Investigate substantiated direct and indirect ownership, ultimate control, voting rights, board influence, contractual arrangements, and other meaningful mechanisms of strategic control as relevant.
- Follow substantiated ultimate control through Hong Kong, offshore holding companies, or other intermediaries where relevant; registration or headquarters location alone does not establish or rule out PRC-capital control.
- Do not infer Chinese control solely from manufacturing or assembly in China, a Chinese-language brand name, ethnicity, or a non-controlling minority investment.
- If ownership or effective control cannot be established with reasonable confidence, **do not include the brand in primary recommendations**. It may appear separately as an explicitly unverified candidate.
- Use proportionate evidence such as official corporate records, credible disclosures, and independent reporting to establish reasonable confidence; exhaustive tracing of every minority shareholder is unnecessary. If reasonable evidence remains insufficient, keep the brand unverified rather than treating silence as clearance.
- Lack of ownership evidence is not evidence of Chinese control. Do not invent a conclusion from an unclear corporate structure.
- When no eligible products are found, explain the constraint instead of silently relaxing it or filling recommendations with unverified candidates.
- Research user-specified brands and products objectively even when they are ineligible for proactive purchase recommendations.
- Assess privacy, security, quality, and reliability separately from ownership-based purchase eligibility; do not imply wrongdoing solely because a brand is excluded.

## Purchase research and comparisons (confirmed P01–P05)

- **P01 — Local availability and support:** Prefer options realistically obtainable in Taiwan with verifiable warranty and after-sales support, while respecting explicit overseas purchasing requests. Distinguish authorized local distribution, parallel imports, and material uncertainty about coverage.
- **P02 — Upgrade value:** When relevant existing equipment is known, compare real practical benefits, compatibility, drawbacks, and incremental costs against that baseline rather than treating higher specifications as sufficient justification.
- **P03 — Independent evidence:** For consequential purchases, seek independent tests, firsthand experiences, recurrent defects, and longer-term reliability information. Distinguish isolated anecdotes from corroborated patterns.
- **P04 — Connected-product risks:** Where a product has network or app functionality, assess relevant cloud dependencies, permissions, data flows, update support, and known vulnerabilities in proportion to the actual exposure and sensitivity.
- **P05 — Brand transparency:** When ownership, origins, or authenticity are unclear, investigate material OEM/ODM or private-label relationships, manufacturing claims, and relevant certifications. Do not infer fraud or relabeling merely from visual resemblance or sparse information.
- Match due diligence to price, connectivity, sensitivity, failure consequences, and the cost of a poor purchase. Give a clear recommendation among verified eligible candidates and keep unverified candidates separate.
- Preserve evidence boundaries: an unverified warranty remains **unknown**, not confirmed absent; one strong feature, benchmark, or integration result does not prove that a product is best overall. Describe the precise demonstrated advantage and what remains unverified.
